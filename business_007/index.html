About APP Realty

More than an agency — consultants, strategists, problem solvers.

APP Realty is an independent, full-service New Jersey brokerage based in Succasunna, Roxbury Township, led by broker Ernest Polej. The firm covers residential and commercial sales, leasing, investment properties, 1031 exchanges and property management across North, Central and South New Jersey — with specialists in multi-family, retail, office and industrial property.
