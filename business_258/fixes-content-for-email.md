What this redesign fixes From listings on other sites to listings on the firm's own site Built from a direct check of the current Elevation Real Estate & Property Group website on 8 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing Homepage is brochure text + subscribe form, zero listings/prices; both the nav 'Current Listings' link and the in-page 'Our Current Listings' button send buyers to an external Realtor.com agent profile (Gemma Mottern) — external-portal-only, nothing on the firm's own site

 02 — Why it costs leads When listings live on other sites, the buyer contacts whoever answers there — not the firm that did the work of winning the listing.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept puts the homes on the firm's own site, under its own name, with its own phone, email and lead form beside them.
