What this redesign fixes From listings on other sites to listings on the firm's own site Built from a direct check of the current Harr Homes / Realiant Real Estate website on 8 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing No search; Buy-page carousel shows 15 listings with prices/photos, but every 'SEE LISTING' link is broken — malformed URL (harrhomesgr.com/https://www.flexmls.com/share/...) that 404s.

 02 — Why it costs leads When listings live on other sites, the buyer contacts whoever answers there — not the firm that did the work of winning the listing.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept puts the homes on the firm's own site, under its own name, with its own phone, email and lead form beside them.
