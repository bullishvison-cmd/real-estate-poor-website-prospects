What this redesign fixes 
 A straight comparison: what is missing on the brokerage's current website, why that costs enquiries from its own clients, and how this concept fixes it. No sales figures are claimed — none were available. 

 1 What&#x27;s missing on the real site No listings anywhere on the site and no listings search or filter — the homepage is story, testimonials and service areas only. There are no listing photos: only a team banner and decorative ivy graphics. This is the one site in this set that does have a working contact form, so lead capture is the one thing that already works. 
 2 Why that costs leads A Starkville buyer — a family relocating for Mississippi State, or a local family moving up — arrives, reads a lovely story about pine trees and ivy, and still cannot see a single home for sale. Without listings, the working form has nothing to attach to: visitors have no home to ask about, no price to react to, and no reason to fill it in. The story builds trust; the missing listings waste it. 
 3 How this redesign fixes it Real Starkville listings now sit above the story, searchable by price, beds, baths and type, each with a photo, price and key facts. The working contact form is kept and improved — pre-filled with the listing a visitor was viewing — so the trust the story builds finally has homes to convert it into enquiries.
