About Pine & Ivy Realty

Jane Poole opened Pine & Ivy Realty with the idea that a brokerage's name should represent 'home'. Her childhood home — tall southern pines, waist-high fences covered in English ivy, neighbours talking over the fence — gave the company its name and its approach: stability, safety and comfort. The office is at 113 Woodcrest Drive, Starkville, open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Mississippi License #23954.
