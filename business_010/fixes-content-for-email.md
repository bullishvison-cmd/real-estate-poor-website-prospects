What this redesign fixes 
 A straight comparison: what is missing on the brokerage's current website, why that costs enquiries from its own clients, and how this concept fixes it. No sales figures are claimed — none were available. 

 1 What&#x27;s missing on the real site No listings at all on the homepage and no search or filter — &#x27;Buy Land&#x27;, &#x27;Buy a Home&#x27; and &#x27;Buy Commercial&#x27; are text-only sections with no properties under them. There are no listing photos: only one generic image on the whole homepage. There is no contact or lead-capture form anywhere — only phone and email text. 
 2 Why that costs leads A Brewton buyer looking for a home, a building lot or new construction arrives and finds nothing to look at — no prices, no photos, no way to narrow by budget or bedrooms. Land buyers especially need acreage and price before they will call, and without a form, even a motivated visitor has to pick up the phone or write an email from scratch. The listings exist — they are simply invisible here. 
 3 How this redesign fixes it Real homes and land appear as searchable cards with photos, prices, beds, baths and acreage, filterable by price, beds, baths and type. A short form (&#x27;I&#x27;m looking for…&#x27;) captures buyers at the moment they find a home they like, and each listing carries a clear next step instead of a dead end.
