What this redesign fixes From a brochure page to a working shopfront Built from a direct check of the current Freedom Realty website on 5 November 2026. 

 01 — What's missing A homepage that is only an agent roster The homepage shows the agent roster and nothing else — no listings, no photos of homes, no search and no lead form. A buyer looking for a Maine home sees faces, not houses. 
 02 — Why it costs leads Maine buyers shop by land and lifestyle A buyer choosing between a Saco in-town home and a Cornish property with 3.5 acres and five outbuildings needs to see the land, the buildings and the price. With no homes on the site, that buyer finds Freedom's own listing — 1783 North Rd — on Zillow instead, and contacts whoever answers there. 
 03 — How this fixes it Homes first, roster second This concept leads with searchable homes — including the verified 1783 North Rd, Cornish at $375,000 — with acreage and outbuildings called out, and keeps the agent team one click away in About, with phone, email and a lead form on every page.
