What this redesign fixes From listings on other sites to listings on the firm's own site Built from a direct check of the current OBA & Associates website on 8 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing The parent site has no listings, prices or search, and its Realty card sends buyers to a separate domain, obarealtygroup.com, which also has no listings or search — its 'FIND A GEORGIA PROPERTY' button just opens a contact form.

 02 — Why it costs leads When listings live on other sites, the buyer contacts whoever answers there — not the firm that did the work of winning the listing.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept puts the homes on the firm's own site, under its own name, with its own phone, email and lead form beside them.
