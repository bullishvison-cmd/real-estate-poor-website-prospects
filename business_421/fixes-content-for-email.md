What this redesign fixes From a static list to a search a buyer can use Built from a direct check of the current The Tate Brokerage website on 8 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing Homepage states 'There's no search box here yet, on purpose' and its Listings link is only an anchor to the same page; the only homes shown are sold examples ($5.3M, $5.7M).

 02 — Why it costs leads A buyer who must scroll one long static list to find anything will give up before they find the home that fits.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept adds a working search — keyword, type, price, beds and baths — with sorting, so a buyer finds the fit in seconds.
