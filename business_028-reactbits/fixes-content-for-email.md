What this redesign fixes Three gaps on the current site — and the fix Based on a review of the brokerage's current homepage on 5 November 2026. This concept shows the change, not a claim about the brokerage's sales. 
 What's missing: a text-only homepage with nothing to buy The current homepage is text only — zero listings, zero photos, zero search. There is no email, phone or contact form on the homepage, only a street address, and the Hours fields are blank. Why that costs leads: the pitch is good, but there's nothing to click The firm's own words make a strong case — boutique service, full MLS access, major-site exposure. But a buyer who believes the pitch finds no home to view and no way to contact anyone from the homepage. The argument ends where the enquiry should begin. How this fixes it: the pitch, with homes and a phone number attached The redesign keeps the boutique story, then immediately shows searchable listings with photos and prices, the office phone and email in the header, and a lead form that reaches Corry directly. Hours are shown as a real schedule instead of blank fields — or removed until confirmed.
