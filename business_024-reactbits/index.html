Our story

Representing the farmers, landowners and families of south central Kansas

Riggin & Company is a Kansas brokerage with 47 years of combined experience and hundreds of sales and auctions behind it. Owner Rusty Riggin is an Accredited Land Consultant who grew up farming and ranching — land is not a product line, it is his life.

The firm handles traditional sales, auctions, 1031 exchanges, mineral rights, site selection and more — statewide, and centred on south central Kansas.
