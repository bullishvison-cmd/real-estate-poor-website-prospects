What this redesign fixes From listings without prices or photos to homes a buyer can compare Built from a direct check of the current Upstate Country Realty website on 5 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing Listings are photo-only tiles: no prices, addresses, captions, beds/baths; current vs sold indistinguishable No search box, filter, or Listings/Search link anywhere; single long homepage Prices exist only 2 clicks deep on individual property pages (verified: 11646 Co Hwy 21 $200,000; 11 Cornell Walk $879,000)

 02 — Why it costs leads A buyer cannot compare homes without prices or photos. With neither, there is nothing to choose between — so they choose another brokerage.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept puts a photo and a price on every card, with beds/baths and type, so buyers can compare homes before they call.
