What this redesign fixes From a brochure page to a working shopfront Built from a direct check of the current Momentus Real Estate Group website on 8 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing Homepage and Buyers page are guidance/process copy only ('People first, houses second'); no homes, prices, photos or search anywhere.

 02 — Why it costs leads A buyer who cannot see a single home, price or photo will not call to ask — they move on to the next brokerage whose listings they can browse.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept leads with searchable homes — photos, prices, beds/baths and type filters — plus phone, email and a lead form on every page.
