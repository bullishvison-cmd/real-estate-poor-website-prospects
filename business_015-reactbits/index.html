About Plaza Realty RGV

Serving the Valley since 1979

Rogelio (Roy) Rivera has been a real estate broker since 1979, and Plaza Realty works across general commercial real estate, open land and residential property in the Rio Grande Valley. Raised in the Valley, Roy has worked with state and out-of-state developers as well as Mexican developers on retail, auto dealership, theatre and other commercial projects — and with local entrepreneurs buying developed or undeveloped property of any size.

The office sits at 5101 S. McColl Road in Edinburg, at the intersection of N. McColl Road and Alberta Road, serving McAllen, Edinburg, Mission, Weslaco, Pharr, San Juan and the surrounding counties.
