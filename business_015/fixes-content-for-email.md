What this redesign fixes 
 What's missing today - and what changes 
 Built from the brokerage's current website, checked November 2026. No sales figures are claimed anywhere on this page: the point is what a visitor can and cannot do today. 

 01 What’s missing No listings and no way to search. The current homepage is a short bio plus value icons — no property is shown, there is no search or filter, and there is no contact form. Typos on the page and a Gmail address sit uneasily beside a broker in business since 1979. 
 02 Why that costs leads A Rio Grande Valley buyer who lands on the site cannot see a single home, price or photo, so they have nothing to act on. With no form, the only next step is to find the phone number or email in the footer — many visitors will simply go back to a portal instead. 
 03 How this fixes it This concept puts searchable listings first — price, type and bedroom filters, real Pharr, Alamo and Donna listings with photos — and a short lead form, so a buyer can go from search to enquiry without leaving the page.
