Removed from the business_024 demo on 2026-11-06 at Gautam's instruction.
Use in the email pitch, not on the demo page.

What this redesign fixes Three gaps on the current site — and the fix Based on a review of the brokerage's current homepage on 5 November 2026. This concept shows the change, not a claim about the brokerage's sales. 
 What's missing: the homepage shows only sold properties The headline section is 'Properties We Have Sold' — three properties, all with images dated April 2022. There is no current listing, no search, and no form on the homepage; 'Find a Property' is only a navigation link. Why that costs leads: buyers can't buy what's already sold A land buyer landing on the site sees proof the firm sells land — and nothing to buy. They have no way to check what is available, what it costs, or how many acres it is, so they leave to search a portal. The firm's active listing, 13000 E 53rd St N at $399,500, is invisible on its own homepage. How this fixes it: current listings first, sold as proof second The redesign leads with the active $399,500 Wichita land listing and a working search by price and acreage. The three sold properties stay — but as a 'Recently sold' proof section, where sold homes belong. Auction vs. traditional sale guidance stays, now beside something a buyer can actually buy.
