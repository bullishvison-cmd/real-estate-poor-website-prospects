What this redesign fixes From a brochure page to a working shopfront Built from a direct check of the current Summit Realty website on 5 November 2026. 

 01 — What's missing No listings at all — and all stock photos The site has services, FAQs and a contact section, but not one home for sale, no search, and every homepage image is a generic Pexels stock photo. There is also no phone number published anywhere — only an email address. 
 02 — Why it costs leads Buyers can't buy what they can't see A Pittsfield buyer comparing agencies will open three sites and call the one whose homes they liked. With no homes and no phone number, Summit asks a buyer to email blind — and most won't. 
 03 — How this fixes it Real homes, real photos, one-tap contact This concept adds a searchable listings grid with photo-led cards, keeps the firm's services and FAQs, and makes the email address prominent on every page — with a lead form so a buyer can enquire in under a minute. A published phone number would strengthen it further.
