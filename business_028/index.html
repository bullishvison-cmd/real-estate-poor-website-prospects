Our story

You come first

Many assume a 'boutique' firm caters only to high-end clients, or is too small for a competitive market. In reality, NextGen LLC Realtors is a specialised business with a culture centred on customer service and professionalism — from a quaint condo to a sprawling estate.

Clients get the same Multiple Listing Service representation as larger companies, plus listing on My State MLS at no extra charge — a nationwide service that gives listings extra coverage. Your property appears on REALTOR.com, Homes.com, Zillow, Trulia and more.
