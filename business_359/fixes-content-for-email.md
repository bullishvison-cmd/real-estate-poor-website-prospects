What this redesign fixes From listings without prices or photos to homes a buyer can compare Built from a direct check of the current Property Shoppe, LLC website on 8 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing No search and no browsable listings — only 3 static featured cards (1 for sale, 1 leased, 1 sold) with no prices on list pages; single for-sale detail (Brookside, $429,000) is the only home a buyer can view.

 02 — Why it costs leads A buyer cannot compare homes without prices or photos. With neither, there is nothing to choose between — so they choose another brokerage.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept puts a photo and a price on every card, with beds/baths and type, so buyers can compare homes before they call.
