What this redesign fixes From listings on other sites to listings on the firm's own site Built from a direct check of the current Taylor Heritage Homes website on 8 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing Polished modern homepage, zero listings/prices anywhere; /buy is brochure-only (services, testimonial, FAQ); both the homepage hero 'SEARCH FOR HOMES' button and /buy's 'Search homes' link open heyleo.com/search in a new tab — the only search route is an external portal; footer disclaimer: 'Home search results are provided through our search partner.'

 02 — Why it costs leads When listings live on other sites, the buyer contacts whoever answers there — not the firm that did the work of winning the listing.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept puts the homes on the firm's own site, under its own name, with its own phone, email and lead form beside them.
