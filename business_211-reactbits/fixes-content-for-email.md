Use in the email pitch, not on the demo page.

What this redesign fixes From an empty listings page to homes a buyer can see Built from a live-browser check of the current Bottari & Associates Realty, Inc. website on 7 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing The “OUR FEATURED LISTINGS” section shows only category tabs (Commercial / Lots & Land / Farms & Ranch / Residential / Rural Residential) with zero listing cards, photos or prices; the top hero renders as a blank grey band and agent photos are missing or broken; a long text wall with a “Commerical” typo; old RealtyProIDX-style template.

 02 — Why it costs leads A buyer who opens the listings page and finds it empty assumes there is nothing for sale — and leaves.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept fills the empty listings page with searchable homes — photos, prices and filters — so the first screen shows what is for sale.
