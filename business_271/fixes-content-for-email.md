# What this fixes - removed from the demo, for the pitch email

Removed from the business_271 demo on 2026-11-08 at Gautam's instruction.
Use in the email pitch, not on the demo page.

What this redesign fixes From a static list to a search a buyer can use Built from a direct check of the current Live Florida Realty website on 8 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing Homepage is brochure + testimonials, no listings/prices; /buy, after a 10-second wait, is brochure-only ('Let's Find Your Next Home / Explore current listings and schedule a private tour') with only a 'Talk to a Local Agent' (contact) button — zero listings, zero prices, no search form or link anywhere; nav only Buy / Sell / About / Contact Us

 02 — Why it costs leads A buyer who must scroll one long static list to find anything will give up before they find the home that fits.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept adds a working search — keyword, type, price, beds and baths — with sorting, so a buyer finds the fit in seconds.
