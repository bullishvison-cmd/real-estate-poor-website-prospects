What this redesign fixes 
 What's missing today - and what changes 
 Built from the brokerage's current website, checked November 2026. No sales figures are claimed anywhere on this page: the point is what a visitor can and cannot do today. 

 01 What’s missing A homepage of plain text only — 43 lines, zero listings, zero photos, no search and no contact form. 
 02 Why that costs leads A San Tan Valley buyer or renter sees no home, no price and no photo, so there is nothing to enquire about. The firm’s real work — current rentals like the Pinnacle Ridge home and recent sales — is invisible, and the visitor has to leave the site to find it. 
 03 How this fixes it This concept shows real homes and rentals with photos, beds, price and status filters, and a lead form — so a buyer or renter can find a fit and ask about it without leaving the page.
