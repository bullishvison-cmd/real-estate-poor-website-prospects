What this redesign fixes From listings on other sites to listings on the firm's own site Built from a direct check of the current Daxsells Realty and Management Co. website on 8 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing Homepage and Buyers page have no homes, prices, photos or on-site search; the Buyers page's 'Search properties' link sends buyers to realtor.com (external-portal-only).

 02 — Why it costs leads When listings live on other sites, the buyer contacts whoever answers there — not the firm that did the work of winning the listing.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept puts the homes on the firm's own site, under its own name, with its own phone, email and lead form beside them.
