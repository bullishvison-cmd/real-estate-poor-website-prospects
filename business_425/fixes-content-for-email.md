What this redesign fixes From listings on other sites to listings on the firm's own site Built from a direct check of the current Charles Poag Realty website on 8 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing Homepage shows only two priced listings, both commercial, and the Listings page has 11 total with only 2 Active (both commercial, $649,900 and $259,900), 9 sold and no active homes; its Search page is a lead form that sends buyers to Zillow.

 02 — Why it costs leads When listings live on other sites, the buyer contacts whoever answers there — not the firm that did the work of winning the listing.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept puts the homes on the firm's own site, under its own name, with its own phone, email and lead form beside them.
