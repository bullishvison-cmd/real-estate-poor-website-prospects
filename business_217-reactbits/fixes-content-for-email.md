Use in the email pitch, not on the demo page.

What this redesign fixes From plain-text listings to homes a buyer can compare Built from a live-browser check of the current Berney Realty, Ltd. website on 7 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing A very dated RealtyProIDX layout (cream/orange/blue); search fields expose raw field names (“Min[Number][LD_List_Price]”, “Min[Decimal][LD_Bathrooms_Total]”); missing spaces (“over 55years!”, “forSELLERS”, “forBUYERS”); a fax number and a toll-free number listed. (Note: four listings with photos and prices do exist, presented in plain-text style.)

 02 — Why it costs leads A buyer cannot compare homes when the search shows raw field names and the listings read as plain text — so they compare on another brokerage's site instead.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept gives every home a photo, a price and the basics on a clean, current layout, with a search that works.
