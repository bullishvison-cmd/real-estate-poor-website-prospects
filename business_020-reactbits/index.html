About Arizona Home Brokerage

A local brokerage, built on trust

Founded in 2013 and led by owner and designated broker Mark Smith — licensed since 2003 — Arizona Home Brokerage is a locally owned, independent, family- and veteran-owned firm rooted in the Southeast Valley. The team lives and works in the communities it serves, specialising in residential buying, selling and property management.

The firm holds SRS, RENE and C2EX designations and is a member of the National Association of Residential Property Managers, serving Gilbert, Chandler, Queen Creek, Mesa, San Tan Valley and surrounding communities.
