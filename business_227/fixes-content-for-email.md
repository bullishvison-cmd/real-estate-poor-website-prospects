What this redesign fixes From a services page with no homes to listings a buyer can browse Built from a direct check of the current Tommy Tucker Realty Co. website on 7 November 2026.

 01 — What's missing No homes for sale on the homepage at all — only service text and links away from the site; the one property shown is a rental already marked RENTED.

 02 — Why it costs leads A buyer who cannot browse a single home from the homepage will not call to ask what is for sale — they move on to a brokerage whose homes they can see.

 03 — How this fixes it This concept puts homes for sale on the first screen — photos, prices, beds/baths and type filters — plus phone and a lead form on every page.
